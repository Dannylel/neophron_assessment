The lighthouse keeper woke before dawn and climbed the spiral stairs, counting each step out of habit. There were one hundred and twelve, the same as yesterday.

Outside, the fog rolled in thick and grey from the bay. Somewhere beyond it a ship's horn sounded twice, low and patient, then fell silent.

He polished the great lens with a soft cloth until it gleamed. The gulls circled overhead, complaining about the weather as they always did.
